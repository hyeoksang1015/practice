## Brand & Style

The design system projects dependable corporate authority balanced with refined sustainable editorial sensitivity. Designed for high-volume enterprise procurement, ESG officers, and luxury supply chain directors, the interface rejects clichéd greenwashing tropes in favor of restrained, archival elegance.

The visual style unites **Minimalism** with an **Editorial Modern** architecture. Generous white space, deliberate architectural grids, and quiet paper-derived backgrounds frame data-dense technical specifications and product catalogs with clarity. The aesthetic experience reflects tactile papercraft: unhurried, structural, and enduring.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-level corporate interactions in tactile, organic tones:

- **Primary (`#183D32`)**: Deep Forest Green. Serves as the primary anchor for dominant structural headers, brand presence, primary interactive controls, and authoritative data readouts.
- **Secondary (`#A8B8A0`)**: Sage Green. Deployed for supporting interface elements, secondary tags, inactive structural highlights, and secondary progress indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#C6CE9B`)**: Muted Olive. Reserved for precise focus indicators, system badges, sustainability score highlights, and active state callouts.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F6F0`)**: Warm Ivory. The default root surface across all pages, emulating unbleached raw paper stock.
- **Neutral Container (`#EAECE7`)**: Sand Tint. Used for secondary surfaces, technical spec tables, card bodies, and inset panels.
- **Structural Stroke (`#DCDFD8`)**: Subtle border tint used for hairline grid dividers and structural containment without visual clutter.
- **Typography Tokens**:
  - Main Text: `#242824` (Charcoal) for high-contrast legibility.
  - Subdued / Secondary: `#5A625A` for secondary copy and captions.
  - Muted: `#717B71` for table metadata, placeholders, and technical timestamps.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial tension between the institutional weight of Noto Serif and the technical, modern efficiency of Noto Sans.

- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (`Noto Serif`)**: Reserved for primary hero statements, institutional manifestos, and section titles. Brings classic craft, trust, and permanence to B2B engagements.
- **Body, UI Controls, & Metrics (`Noto Sans`)**: Optimized for technical specification matrices, packaging volume calculators, order forms, and enterprise navigation.
- Letter spacing remains tight on editorial headlines to emphasize architectural density, while small UI labels leverage expanded tracking for scannability across enterprise dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a structured 12-column grid system framed by generous outer canvases:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with `2rem` gutters and `4rem` outer margins. Max content boundary is capped at `1440px` to maintain comfortable eye-tracking lengths on editorial text blocks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Complex inventory and spec tables reflow into vertical comparison cards.

Vertical rhythm follows disciplined spacing bands:
- Component-level spacing relies on micro increments (`space-xs` through `space-md`).
- Sectional transitions in editorial narrative templates leverage `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` to preserve white space and avoid visual density.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects heavy drop shadows, skeuomorphic glows, and floating layers. Depth is constructed purely through **tonal layers** and **low-contrast outlines**:

- **Hairline Framing**: Structural separation is achieved via `1px solid #DCDFD8` rules. Cards, drawers, and headers rely on perimeter lines rather than cast shadows.
- **Surface Layering**:
  - Base Level: Warm Ivory (`#F7F6F0`).
  - Elevated Container / Secondary Panel: Sand Tint (`#EAECE7`).
  - Inset Fields / Active Focus Wells: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) framed by Deep Forest Green (`#183D32`).
- **State Overlays**: Interactive elements shift tonal weight upon hover rather than lifting on the z-axis. A hover state translates to background color shifts (e.g., `#EAECE7` transitioning to `#E2E5DF`) paired with crisp border darkening (`#C2C7BD`).

## Shapes

The design system enforces a soft, architectural geometry (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Default Form & Container Elements**: Radius of `0.25rem` (4px). Standardized for inputs, technical data modules, card wrappers, and structural containers, providing crisp discipline.
- **Large Sections & Inset Panels (`rounded-lg`)**: Radius of `0.5rem` (8px). Used for modal dialogs and media containers.
- **Interactive Badges & Pill Buttons**: Buttons and status chips may use full-bleed pill profiles (`9999px`) where an explicit call-to-action needs to contrast against the rigid 12-column rectangular frame.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#183D32`, text `#F7F6F0`, border `1px solid transparent`. Pill-shaped or soft `0.25rem` radius. In hover state, transitions to `#234F42`. Active state deepens to `#0E261F`.
- **Secondary**: Transparent background, text `#183D32`, border `1px solid #183D32`. Hover fills with `#EAECE7`.
- **Tertiary / Text**: Subtle underlined link styling in `#183D32` with `Noto Sans` medium weight.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F6F0`, height `48px`, border `1px solid #DCDFD8`, text `#242824`.
- Focus state: `1px solid #183D32` with an ambient `0 0 0 1px #183D32` outline ring. No colored glow.
- Helper and placeholder text: `#717B71`.
- Error state: `1px solid #8A382C` with contextual error note in `#8A382C`.

### Cards & Technical Modules
- Background `#EAECE7` or `#F7F6F0` wrapped with `1px solid #DCDFD8`.
- Padding: `1.5rem` on desktop, `1rem` on mobile.
- Headers inside cards feature an understated border-bottom (`1px solid #DCDFD8`) to segregate metadata from body copy.

### Chips & Sustainability Badges
- Height: `24px` to `28px`.
- Shape: Rounded pill (`9999px`).
- Background `#EAECE7`, text `#183D32`, border `1px solid #DCDFD8`.
- High-priority / Certified badges: Background `#C6CE9B` with `#183D32` text.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Box dimension: `18px x 18px`.
- Unchecked: Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #717B71`.
- Checked: Background `#183D32`, border `1.5px solid #183D32`, checkmark icon in `#F7F6F0`.

### Data Tables (Technical Spec & Inventory)
- Cell padding: `12px 16px`.
- Row borders: Hairline divider `1px solid #DCDFD8`.
- Table Header: Text in `label-sm` uppercase, color `#5A625A`, background `#EAECE7`.
- Alternating rows avoid striped zebra fills; hover state triggers `#F0F2ED`.

### Corporate Entity Placeholders
- Corporate placeholders must strictly adhere to bracketed institutional masks: `[COMPANY NAME]`, `[BUSINESS ADDRESS]`, `[TAX IDENTIFIER]`, and `[CONTACT REPRESENTATIVE]`.
