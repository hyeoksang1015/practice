## Brand & Style

The design system projects high-output athletic energy through a disciplined, premium, and starkly modern digital environment. Tailored for dedicated urban athletes, coaches, and club members, the interface mirrors the precision, focus, and technical authority of contemporary sportswear editorial campaigns.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Expression
- **Technical Neo-Brutalist Minimalism**: Rooted in high-contrast athletic editorial layouts, pairing severe structural discipline with dynamic, kinetic accents. 
- **Industrial Precision**: Heavy reliance on clear technical indices, sharp numeric identifiers (`01`, `02`, `03`), monospaced technical metadata, and precise horizontal and vertical dividing rules.
- **Asymmetric Tension**: Dynamic full-bleed hero zones juxtaposed with tight, metric-dense workout trackers and architectural content grids. Imagery avoids generic fitness lifestyle tropes, favoring high-shutter-speed athletic movement, monochrome depth, and high-contrast flash photography.
- **Strict Tone Constraints**: Strictly zero pastel tones, zero earthy warm beige, and zero organic olive greens. Every visual surface is calibrated around high-voltage performance neutrals and electric color accents.

## Colors

The palette relies on deep architectural blacks to establish an athletic stage, illuminated by high-intensity electric blue and acid lime for critical telemetry and callouts.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#5265F5` - Electric Blue)**: The core dynamic driver. Used for primary actions, current session indicators, active tab markers, and primary focus rings.
- **Secondary (`#D8FF3E` - Acid Lime)**: High-voltage telemetry accent. Reserved for real-time status updates, live class badges, heart rate zones, target achievement highlights, and urgent booking alerts. Never used for large body surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#F2F0EA` - Off-White)**: High-clarity editorial text and primary headline ink. Provides intense contrast against deep surfaces without the harsh glare of pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Neutral (`#17191F` - Charcoal Black)**: The primary canvas floor. Paired with tinted charcoal steps (`#1F222A`, `#272B35`, `#323744`) to construct structured modular layers.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Reserved exclusively for high-contrast badge icons, pure white stat callouts, and inverted primary states.

### Contrast Discipline
- Primary text on charcoal canvas uses `#F2F0EA` (contrast ratio exceeds 13:1).
- Interactive `#D8FF3E` elements always take dark ink (`#17191F`) for absolute legibility.
- Subordinate metadata and section indexing use `#8F94A6` against dark backgrounds to enforce strict visual rank.

## Typography

The typographic system fuses the bold, geometric impact of Manrope for display headlines with the neutral, engineered legibility of Inter for body copy and dense telemetry.

### Typographic Hierarchies & Editorial Rules
- **Headline Tracking & Weights**: Display and Hero sizes carry negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to generate condensed, urgent, commercial-sportswear headlines. Headlines must lean into compact structural capitalization.
- **Section Index Markers**: System indicators, section numbering (e.g., `[ 01 // ROSTER ]`), and performance tags utilize `label-numeric` and `label-caps` in all-caps with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`).
- **Korean Localization**: Inter and Manrope map smoothly alongside modern Korean grotesque systems (Pretendard). Korean body copy adheres to `-0.02em` letter-spacing with a line-height multiplier of 1.6x for clear technical legibility across mobile viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are constructed on an architectural 12-column grid system (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet) calibrated for athletic pacing and editorial impact.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rhythm
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `margin-sm` (1rem) and `gutter-sm` (1rem). Dense vertical stacked layouts, pinned sticky bottom session reservation docks, and edge-to-edge carousel modules.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `margin` (1.5rem) and `gutter` (1.5rem). Split stat panels and 2-column class schedules.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with `margin-lg` (3rem) and `gutter-lg` (2rem). Content bounds cap at 1440px for editorial control, featuring asymmetric column balancing (e.g., 5-column sticky metadata index alongside 7-column media feed).

### Spatial Discipline
Internal component spacing follows strict 4px/8px increments. Compact layouts (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) govern workout stats and metric tables, while expansive macro-rhythms (`space-xl` and above) bracket high-impact hero zones and editorial imagery breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial skeuomorphic drop shadows in favor of tonal layering, razor-sharp structural outlines, and selective back-lit luminescence.

### Tonal Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas (`#17191F`)**: Deepest ground layer for overall application framing.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#1F222A`)**: Base modules, non-active schedule blocks, background table rows.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#272B35`)**: Hovered tiles, elevated interactive cards, drawer menus.
- **Surface Level 3 (`#323744`)**: Floating dialogs, metric tooltips, pinned floating reservation docks.

### Outlines & Border Definition
Depth is articulated primarily via precision hairpins. All cards and panel boundaries feature a solid `1px` border with `rgba(242, 240, 234, 0.08)`. Active states upgrade borders directly to `#5265F5` or `#D8FF3E`.

### Atmospheric Energy & Glow
Floating performance elements and live status indicators employ restrained, directional luminescence:
- Electric Glow: `0px 8px 24px rgba(82, 101, 245, 0.25)`
- Acid Indicator Pulse: `0px 0px 16px rgba(216, 255, 62, 0.35)`

## Shapes

The design system enforces a soft-precision corner geometry (`roundedness: 1`), generating an architectural, high-performance aesthetic.

### Radius Scale
- **Base (0.25rem / 4px)**: Buttons, text input fields, status chips, segmented controls, table containers.
- **Large (0.5rem / 8px)**: Editorial image wrappers, session detail cards, modal dialogue panels.
- **Extra Large (0.75rem / 12px)**: Large canvas panels and workout dashboards.
- **Strict Pill Exception**: Exclusively reserved for ultra-compact telemetry tags, circular icon badges, and live indicator dots (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic**: Solid `#5265F5` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Uppercase `label-caps` typography, 4px border radius. Hover shifts to `#3F51E0` with an electric glow. Active press scales down slightly (`scale(0.98)`).
- **High-Alert / Action (Acid)**: Solid `#D8FF3E` with `#17191F` bold typography. Strictly applied to primary conversions (e.g., "BOOK CLASS", "CHECK IN").
- **Secondary / Ghost**: `#17191F` surface with a crisp `1px` border in `rgba(242, 240, 234, 0.2)`. Text in `#F2F0EA`. Hover state illuminates border to `#5265F5`.
- **Destructive**: Subdued dark charcoal surface with `#FF4B4B` border and typography.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips**: 4px radius, compact padding (`4px 8px`). Built with dark charcoal background (`#1F222A`) and a `1px` perimeter line. Live classes incorporate a pulsing 6px `#D8FF3E` circular indicator dot.
- **Index Tag**: Square-edged aesthetic, monospace-styled typography indicating workout zones (e.g., `ZONE 03`, `HIIT 45`).

### Input Fields
- Dark container fill (`#1F222A`) with `1px` border (`rgba(242, 240, 234, 0.12)`). Label positioned above field using `label-caps` in `#8F94A6`.
- **Focus State**: Border sharpens instantly to `#5265F5`, backed by a subtle 2px glow ring (`rgba(82, 101, 245, 0.2)`). No soft outlines.

### Cards & Modular Grids
- Constructed on `#1F222A` surfaces with `1px` boundary borders (`rgba(242, 240, 234, 0.08)`).
- Integrated index counters at the top right of cards (`01`, `02`).
- Asymmetric image cropping: Photos inside cards adhere to 4:5 or 16:9 ratios with high-contrast monochrome treatments that tint to full spectrum upon hover.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes**: 4px radius, 20px square. Unchecked: `1px` border in `#8F94A6` with charcoal interior. Checked: Solid `#5265F5` with high-contrast white vector checkmark.
- **Segmented Schedule Switcher**: Pinned surface in `#1F222A`, active segment highlighted via solid `#272B35` tab and sharp `#D8FF3E` bottom accent border.

### Athletic Telemetry & Stat Modules
- Numeric-first layout: Giant Manrope figures (`display-hero` or `headline-xl`) paired directly above compact tracking micro-labels (`label-caps`).
- Performance Progress Bars: Ultra-thin 4px tracks in `#272B35` filled with solid `#5265F5` or `#D8FF3E`.
